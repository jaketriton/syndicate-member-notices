Text message notices for existing members of The Syndicate, operated by The Launch Partnership, LLC. Get timely, informational updates about activity in your member account.

About this program

The Syndicate is a membership community operated by The Launch Partnership, LLC. Members can sign up below to receive text notices about their membership. This SMS program sends informational account notices only to members who sign up below. Topics include:

  • Account update notices
  • Document available notices
  • Event reminders
  • Membership notices

These messages are operational notices about your membership. They are not offers or solicitations and contain no advertising.

Privacy commitment

The Launch Partnership, LLC sends these messages to its own members. We never sell, rent, or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates. See our Privacy Policy.
