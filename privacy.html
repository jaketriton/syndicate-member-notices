Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how The Launch Partnership, LLC ("The Syndicate," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website and our SMS member notices program. Last updated: November 2026.

Information we collect

When you sign up, we collect your name, mobile phone number, optional email address, and a record of your SMS consent (date, time, and the consent language shown).

How we use information

We use this information only to send the member account notices you requested, respond to HELP or support requests, honor opt-outs, and maintain records of consent.

No sharing of mobile information

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or SMS consent with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing or any other purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any text message to opt out. To request access to or deletion of your information, email support@thesyndicate.com.
