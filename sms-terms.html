SMS Terms & Conditions

These SMS Terms apply to The Syndicate Member Account Notices program ("Program") operated by The Launch Partnership, LLC ("we," "us").

Program description

The Program sends informational account notices to members of The Syndicate who opt in on our website, including:

  • Account update notices
  • Document available notices
  • Event reminders
  • Membership notices

Messages are informational only. They do not contain offers, solicitations, or advertising.

How to opt in

You opt in by entering your mobile number on our sign-up form and checking the unchecked SMS consent box. After opting in you will receive a confirmation text. Consent is not a condition of membership or any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on account activity.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details on your plan.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to cancel. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed and will receive no further messages unless you opt in again. You may also email support@thesyndicate.com.

Help

Privacy
